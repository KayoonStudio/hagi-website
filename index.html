See how it works

Headline first, numbers second.

Every day gets one color and a few words that tell you what kind of day it is. The temperature is there when you want it.

  • Feels like 2° Coat weather. Cold
  • Feels like 14° Jacket on. Cool
  • Feels like 24° T-shirt day. Warm

Dressed for the hours you’re actually out.

Hagi looks at the forecast for when you’re outside, not just the daily high. Tell it what today looks like and the outfit follows.

  • Commute, outside all day, or mostly inside
  • Rain, snow, slush and ice, with what to bring
  • When the jacket can come off

It learns how you run.

Some people need a coat at 9°, others are fine in a hoodie. Tap “I’m cold” or “I’m warm” when you feel it, and Hagi adjusts each layer: outer, top, legs, feet, hat and gloves.

  • Gets more confident every day
  • Only suggests clothes you said you own

Your outfit at a glance.

Put today’s outfit on your Home Screen or Lock Screen. A quick look on the way to the door is all it takes.

Answer in one tap.

A morning notification with the outfit, and an evening check-in you can answer right from the notification: too cold, just right, or too warm. Both are optional.

Private by design.

Hagi has no servers of its own. What you tell it stays with you.

No account
Nothing to sign up for, nothing to sign in to.
No tracking
No analytics, no ads, no third-party trackers.
Your iCloud
Feedback and settings sync between your devices through your own iCloud.
No location access
You pick a city. Forecasts come from Open-Meteo.
